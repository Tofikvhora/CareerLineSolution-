Privacy Policy

How CareerLine Solution protects candidate resumes, client intelligence, and personal data under Indian IT regulations and global security standards.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • ISO 9001:2015 Aligned
Overview

1. Commitment to Data Privacy

At CareerLine Solution ("CareerLine", "we", "our", or "us"), we value the trust you place in us when sharing your personal information, resume documents, and professional credentials. As a Pan-India HR consultancy and recruitment service provider headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, we are dedicated to handling your information ethically, securely, and in full compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act).

Zero Candidate Fee Policy: CareerLine Solution strictly adheres to fair recruitment principles. We never charge job seekers or candidates any recruitment, registration, interview, or placement fees. We will never ask for your banking passwords, OTPs, or credit card information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to facilitate recruitment, executive search, career advisory, and HR consulting services:

  • Candidate Profiles & Resumes: Full name, email address, telephone/mobile number, current residence/city, work experience, educational background, technical and managerial skills, current and expected compensation (CTC), notice period, and resume/CV attachments (PDF, DOCX).
  • Corporate Employer Inquiries: Organization name, industry vertical, business email, authorized representative contact details, job vacancy descriptions, hiring budgets, and corporate staffing requirements.
  • Automated & Technical Logs: Browser user agent, timestamp of application, IP address, and security verification logs used exclusively to detect bot automation and unauthorized access.

3. How We Use Candidate Information

Candidate personal information and uploaded CVs are used exclusively for legitimate recruitment purposes:

  • Matching candidate profiles with open job vacancies across IT, BFSI, Manufacturing, Healthcare, BPO, and Executive sectors.
  • Contacting candidates via phone call, WhatsApp, or email to schedule screening interviews and technical evaluations.
  • Submitting vetted candidate profiles to prospective client employers only with the candidate's prior consent.
  • Maintaining an active talent database to notify professionals of future career opportunities matching their domain skills.

4. Resume Storage & Infrastructure Security

We implement industry-grade technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, tampering, disclosure, or destruction of your uploaded files:

  • Encrypted File Ingestion: Resumes submitted through our online portal undergo automated file type verification, MIME signature checks, and sanitized UUID renaming to prevent malware injection.
  • Anti-Automation & Rate Limiting: All submission endpoints are secured with multi-tier rate limiting and bot detection to block automated web crawlers and scraping bots from accessing applicant data.
  • Restricted Recruiter Access: Only authorized CareerLine recruiters and HR managers with cryptographically secured credentials can access submitted CV files.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

CareerLine Solution maintains a strict No-Sale Policy. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade candidate personal data or resume databases to third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or lead generators.

Information is only shared under the following conditions:

  • Vetted Client Employers: With reputable corporate clients who have signed non-disclosure staffing agreements with CareerLine Solution for active job openings.
  • Legal Compliance: When strictly required by law enforcement, court order, or regulatory bodies in accordance with applicable Indian laws.

6. Data Retention & Candidate Rights

Candidates retain full control over their professional information:

  • Retention Period: Resumes and application records are retained in our secure database for up to 24 months to support ongoing talent pipelining, unless a deletion request is received.
  • Right to Review & Update: You may request an updated copy of your resume or correct any inaccurate professional records at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Opt-Out): You may request complete deletion of your resume and contact records from our database by emailing privacy@careerlinesolution.com. Deletion is executed within 7 business days.

7. Cookies & Session Management

Our website uses minimal, essential functional cookies and local storage tokens:

  • Essential Tokens: Used to maintain secure recruiter administrative sessions (JSON Web Tokens).
  • Analytics & Performance: Anonymous, aggregated metrics to monitor website speed, uptime, and search functionality without personal profiling.
